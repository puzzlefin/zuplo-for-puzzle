# What is Puzzle?

<br />

**Puzzle** is modern accounting software built for startups and high-growth companies.\
It gives founders and finance teams real-time visibility into their financial data — without waiting for month-end reports or manual bookkeeping.

***

## The Modern Accounting Platform

Puzzle brings together everything a company needs to stay financially informed:

* **Real-time bookkeeping** powered by automation
* **Dual-basis accounting** — cash and accrual, side by side
* **Instant insights** into cash, burn, runway, and ARR
* **An immutable, audit-ready ledger** designed for accuracy and trust
* **Developer-friendly APIs** that make accounting data programmable

Puzzle isn’t just accounting software — it’s a **live source of financial truth** for your business.

***

## Built for Modern Companies

Traditional systems were designed for accountants. Puzzle is designed for builders — founders, operators, and finance teams who move fast and expect their numbers to keep up.

With Puzzle, your books update automatically as transactions occur. Your financials, metrics, and insights are always current — so you can make decisions with confidence.

> **In short:** Puzzle transforms accounting from something you *do later* into something that’s *always working for you*.
