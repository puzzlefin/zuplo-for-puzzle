# Embedding Puzzle

<br />

Embedding the **Puzzle API** lets you offer modern accounting capabilities inside your own product — from data sync to reporting and workflow automation.

## Capabilities

With the API, you can:

* **Onboard new businesses** into Puzzle directly from your platform.
* **Run accounting operations** such as invoicing, bill payments, or categorizing transactions.
* **Expose reports and statements** generated from Puzzle’s accounting engine.
* **Keep bookkeepers and clients in sync**, just like they would be in Puzzle itself.

## Access Modes

* **Read-only:** Great for analytics, dashboards, or embedded insights.
* **Read-write:** Ideal for accounting platforms or back-office tools that automate bookkeeping or client workflows.

## Why Embed Puzzle

Embedding Puzzle gives your users a native accounting experience — without having to build or maintain an accounting system yourself.
